# Task 0 — Writeup
My strategy with writing this prompt was based on giving the llm an overview of the task ['You are the judge. Your job is to first of all read and ingest the user message.'] then breaking it down into more specific instructions ['...in particular, look for two patterns:...'] and drilling further with instructions where i thought they'd help. Tried to make the prompt as thorough yet general as possible. 
Started by writing something that felt in the right ballpark then iterated with a subsample of the data, trying to avoid pitfalls at each turn and improving the metrics until something suitable was achieved. Then ran on the full dataset and tweaked things a little. Added some cast iron calibration examples at this stage in order to refine the behaviour and try to steer the prompt / llm away from common mistakes that kept happening without overfitting to specific test rows. The 'gray' area that i had to navigate was mainly 'travel adjacent' questions such as about general geography / languages / culture and trying to get the llm to correctly decide if these were appropriately travel related [e.g. questions that look travel-related but are actually general-knowledge — capital of Mongolia, photosynthesis explanations — needed explicit counter-examples].
